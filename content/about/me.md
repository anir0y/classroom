I'm Animesh Roy, known online as anir0y. I work as an offensive security consultant and run an independent security research practice from India. Over more than 13 years in InfoSec my work has covered penetration testing, red teaming, cloud and RF security, hardware hacking and VAPT.

## Why this site exists

Classroom is where I publish my lab write-ups. Each one goes through a room or challenge task by task: the commands, the output, and the reasoning behind each step. The point is that you can take the method to a different target, which a bare list of answers does not let you do.

Most of the write-ups are TryHackMe rooms, grouped into the learning paths below. If you are working through one of those paths, start at room 1 and the site will keep track of where you got to.

## Get in touch

For consulting, training or a question about a write-up, use the links at the top of this page. Corrections are welcome: every post has a "Suggest Changes" link that opens the source on GitHub.
