Why the archive earns almost nothing yet: indexing

Google knows 544 URLs but only 192 are indexed. Everything not indexed cannot earn. The 230 "crawled, not yet indexed" are mostly the 91 August posts waiting their turn, this is lag, not a bug.

StateURLs
Read this as a funnel. 352 URLs are not indexed: 230 crawled but not yet chosen (recent posts, needs internal links + time), 61 not yet crawled, 35 taxonomy pages deliberately noindexed (correct), 20 false 404s (Google misreading shell commands like /bin/bash inside code blocks, harmless), 6 minor.
